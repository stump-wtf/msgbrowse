Scoring sends each conversation's messages to your LLM endpoint in batches and asks the model to score them against the IPIP lexicon — this is the most expensive pass msgbrowse runs. {{num .Build.Remaining}} conversations have not been scored under this model yet, and each one costs at least one call. It resumes from where the last run stopped and re-scores nothing already paid for. {{- if or (gt .Build.Processed 0) (gt .Build.Scores 0)}} Rescore discards all {{num .Build.Scores}} stored scores and pays for every one of the {{num .Build.Conversations}} conversations again.{{end}}

Scores describe what a message expresses, not the person who wrote it — they are AI-generated indications, not a psychological assessment. Contacts can opt out on their profile, which deletes their stored scores and keeps their messages out of every future run.
